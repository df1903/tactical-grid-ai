\section{Docstrings y Validación Estudiantil}
\label{sec:docstrings}

Cada función/método relevante tiene docstring con:
\begin{itemize}
\item \code{Purpose}: Responsabilidad concreta
\item \code{Preconditions}: Condiciones previas
\item \code{Postconditions}: Condiciones posteriores
\item \code{Complexity}: Costo temporal/espacial
\item \code{AI usage}: \code{Yes}/\code{No}
\item \code{AI intervention}: Detalle si hubo IA
\item \code{Student validation}: revisión cruzada realizada por los integrantes del equipo
\end{itemize}

Ejemplo:
\begin{minted}{python}
def legal_actions(board: Board, state: GameState) -> list[Action]:
    """List the actions available to the side whose turn it is.

    Purpose: enumerate deterministic legal moves (4 directions) and waits per unit.

        moves to free passable in-map cells that are not the rival base, then Wait.
    Complexity: O(U * 4 * U) with U units (occupancy lookup is linear in U).
    AI usage: Yes (Claude Code drafted from rules.md).
    AI intervention: none after draft; rules decided by team on 2026-10-14.
    Student validation: revisión cruzada realizada por los integrantes del equipo.
    """
